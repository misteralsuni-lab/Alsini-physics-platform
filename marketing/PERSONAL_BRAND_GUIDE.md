# PERSONAL_BRAND_GUIDE.md

Defines who Mahmoud Alsini is *as a brand*, what messages he
stands behind, what his area of expertise actually is (vs what he
refuses to claim), and where the brand is heading over the next
several years.

This document is the *constitution*. The marketing architecture
and the LinkedIn strategy are *operational code* under it.

---

## 1. Professional identity

### The single-sentence identity

> A practising IGCSE/A-Level Physics teacher (Edexcel) who also
> ships a production-grade AI tutor to teach his own craft the
> way he teaches it. Software engineer by training, educator by
> vocation, product-builder by current necessity.

### Five structured identity claims (use these as templates)

1. **By training.** I am a mechanical engineer (University of
   Tripoli, 2021). That undergrad shapes how I think about
   physics: as a system, not as a collection of facts.
2. **By vocation.** I teach — currently IGCSE and A-Level
   Physics, Edexcel-centred, both paper- and lab-facing. The
   PGCEi (LJMU, in flight) is the practitioner-research layer.
3. **By current necessity.** I am building EDU-VLE, a RAG-based
   physics tutor, in public, on a single-worksheet
   Golden-Dataset scope, with a hard rule that I don't ship a
   claim I can't back up.
4. **By non-linearity.** Engineering → teaching → AI is not a
   ladder. It is a *rare intersection* — and that intersection
   is the mode I want to live in.
5. **By refusal.** I refuse two roles: "AI hype-broker" and
   "passionate educator" genericist. If a LinkedIn template
   would make me sound like either, I delete the post.

### Two brands, one person

There are two brands inside a coherent identity:

- **Mahmoud (Track A)** — the practitioner whose trajectory is
  the signal. (Used by recruiters and school leaders.)
- **EDU-VLE (Track B)** — the artifact that demonstrates the
  signal. (Used by peer teachers and EdTech reviewers.)

These are *the same story* from two angles. They never contradict,
and the surface area is small on purpose.

## 2. Key messages (the 6 messages that compound)

These are the six beats Mahmoud's brand plays over 12+ months. No
single post plays them all; a single page (LinkedIn About, banner,
portfolio reader) should compress them.

1. **Pedagogy first, code second.** EDU-VLE is downstream of a
   teaching problem, not an engineering exercise.
2. **Specific beats aspirational.** A named result (pass rate
   +11 points, 21 chunks, 3 visual assets) is always stronger
   than a smooth claim ("revolutionise", "transform", "engage").
   Inauthenticity is a brand-death event.
3. **Build-in-public is the strongest credential.** Hiding the
   bugs erases the proof. Showing the bugs makes the proof.
4. **The non-linear path is the moat.** Engineering graduates
   who became teachers and then shipped AI products is a thin
   population. Lean into it.
5. **Classroom evidence over vendor evidence.** When a vendor
   says "AI improves outcomes," the response is the named
   classroom result; when a teacher says "this works," the
   response is the worksheet, the spec-point, the spec-point
   teardown.
6. **AI-in-education is a craft problem, not a tech problem.**
   The teachers fail EdTech, not the other way around. Pillar 3
   essays live here.

## 4. Long-term positioning (10-year horizon)

### Where the brand is heading

**Mahmoud, in 5–10 years, is recognisable in two distinct circles,
   both of which the current strategy serves:**

1. **British-curriculum international schools (Gulf, Egypt,
   diaspora, possibly UK domestic).** A credible Head-of-Physics
   candidate with EdTech literacy, named for hybrid gcse-plus-
   AI capability.
2. **EdTech-startup founder-engineer pool.** One of the few
   practitioners who ships a *classroom-validated* AI product,
   not a demo. This is the rarer and higher-leverage of the two.

The current strategy must serve both without forcing convergence.
The mechanism: Pillar 1 + voice rules serve (1); Pillar 2 + EDU-VLE
artifacts serve (2).

### EDU-VLE in the long-term picture

Two equally acceptable outcomes — both serve Track A:

- **Outcome A.** EDU-VLE becomes a real product serving a defined
  classroom niche (Gen 1–3 of tutors validating it).
- **Outcome B.** EDU-VLE remains a credible build-in-public
  artifact, and Mahmoud's career goes via head-of-physics +
  EdTech-adjacent product counsel.

The marketing strategy **does not depend** on which outcome
occurs. This is why the positioning is a *doctrine*, not an
aspiration. Aspirational marketing breaks the brand the moment
the aspiration slips.

### What the strategy protects against

- Becoming "just another teacher on LinkedIn" (lost to Pillar 1
  without Pillar 2).
- Becoming "just another demo on the AI EdTech timeline" (lost
  to Pillar 2 without Pillar 1).
- Owning neither: the trap if both are neglected and the brand
  becomes "person with a side-project."

Pillar 1 ↔ Pillar 2 equilibrium is what the calendar enforces.

## 5. Voice (locked, see also MARKETING_ARCHITECTURE.md §7)

### On-voice

Concrete. Calm. Specific. Numbers over vibes. Engineer-clinician
cadence. Educator who happens to ship.

### Off-voice

Hustle-broker cadence, motivational-existence cadence,
emoji-opener cadence, "would love your thoughts" signoffs,
"passionate educator" genericism, "AI-powered personalised
learning" vendorese.

### Cross-line non-negotiables

- British English globally.
- No fabricated claims (marketers commonly fabricate; Mahmoud's
  brand cannot survive it).
- No social handles, no emails, no Telegram QR in any classroom-
  material-style image. (Existing project rule. Brand-consistent
  outside classroom artifacts too: a brand email is fine in a
  portfolio, a handle in a post CTA is not.)
- No TG-personal-channel promotion under guise of marketing
  content.
- No profanity. No political content unless directly EdTech-
  adjacent (One-Off Pillar 3 essay territory).

## 6. Brand-equity checklist (annual, private)

Once a year, run this checklist privately. No published score.

- "Can a recruiter who saw a single Pillar 1 post summarise what
  I do in one sentence?" (Yes / No + the sentence they said.)
- "Can a peer teacher tell another peer teacher why my materials
  are worth their time?" (Yes / No.)
- "Have I posted any claim I cannot back up with a current code
  path, named number, or named document?" (Binary audit on
  every post in the trailing 12 months.)
- "Has my cadence slipped into compensation-mode (back-filling
  after pauses)?" (Yes means reset cadence. No means strategy
  holds.)
- "Does the LinkedIn profile still scan in <8 seconds for an
  unfamiliar recruiter?" (Yes / No + time to read.)

If three or more "No" outcomes: the strategy needs adjustment,
not abandonment. Otherwise stay the course.

## 7. Out-of-scope for this guide

- Visual identity system (logo, palette, type). Deferred until
  Track A or B requires it for an artifact (PDF portfolio,
  workshop slide). Until then, plain professional photos and
  text-only posts.
- Spoken-media play (podcast, YouTube interviews). Not before a
  Pillar 3 essay lands reached a >=5K-impression week.
- Conference presence. Only after ≥3 Pillar 2 posts land at
  >2K impressions; talk proposals are a *target* at
  month 6+, not a strategy deliverable in M1.
- Newsletter platform. Not before profile-to-DM conversion
  ≥2% AND inbound DMs reach ≥1/week.

The guide protects the strategy from growing into itself.
