# CONTENT_CALENDAR.md

12-week content roadmap. One long-form LinkedIn post per week,
alternating Pillar 2 (build-in-public, odd weeks) and Pillar 1
(teaching-craft, even weeks). Pillar 3 essays land at weeks 6 and
10. No back-fill after skips.

Calendar is expressed in **calendar week numbers** with the
**pillar** and **post archetype** locked. The exact topic is
filled in that week, no earlier. This keeps the strategy adaptive
to whatever EDU-VLE session is currently shipping.

---

## Conventions

- **W** = calendar week of the year (1=first week of January).
- **P1** = Pillar 1 (Teaching craft); **P2** = Pillar 2
  (Build-in-public); **P3** = Pillar 3 (AI literacy).
- **A1..A5** = post archetypes from LINKEDIN_STRATEGY.md §2.
- **All Tuesday 08:00 Tripoli** unless an EDU-VLE session week
  triggers a pause (no compensation; reset cadence after).
- The **Carry** row is for the Link A4 Education-observation /
  A3 Career-trajectory posts that land where the annual roadmap
  permits.

## Special events (out-of-band, allowed)

These are NOT counted against the weekly cadence if they happen.
They exist to ensure the loop never breaks under real-world
constraint:

- **EDU-VLE session weeks.** Pause. Resume next week without
  back-fill.
- **LJMU PGCEi assignment weeks.** Pause per same rule.
- **Travel / holiday.** Same rule. No "sorry I've been quiet /
  back with a big one" posts — they read as compensation and
  break voice.
- **A genuine career milestone** (offer received, offer
  rejected, role accepted, public talk accepted). Slot to next
  available Tuesday at Pillar 1, A3 archetype. ≤1x/quarter.

## Forbidden in this calendar (explicit)

- No X/Twitter posts created from the LinkedIn post by default —
  only if the post itself is <100 words and reads natively on
  X. If rewriting takes >5 min, skip the cross-post.
- No newsletter platform launch in 12 weeks. The strategy does
  not need email infrastructure yet.
- No blog / personal-site launch. The LinkedIn profile IS the
  portfolio reader for now.
- No "12-week recap / 12-week lookahead" posts. They read as
  vanity and break voice.
- No metrics reveal post ("here's my engagement %"). The strategy
  refuses these.
- No AI-tool evaluation posts about competitors. They are out
  of voice for Mahmoud's positioning and add nothing to either
  track.

## 12-week measurement snapshot

At W12, log privately:
- W1–W12 post count (target = 12, allow 9–12).
- Skipped weeks and reason (each → either EDU-VLE session, PGCEi,
  or other; track counts but never compensate).
- Inbound DMs (count + 1-line source — which post triggered or
  "cold inbound").
- Profile-view trend from LinkedIn native analytics.
- One qualitative note: which post drew the best reply? Which
  drew the worst reaction? Why?

No dashboard beyond this. The strategy is judged on the previous
three bullets at W12, then re-decided for W13+.
