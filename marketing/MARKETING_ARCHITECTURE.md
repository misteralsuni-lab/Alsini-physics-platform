# MARKETING_ARCHITECTURE.md

A positioning charter for Mahmoud Alsini, supporting EDU-VLE under a
~1-hour-per-week constraint. Strategy, not growth-hacking.

---

## 1. Strategic posture

This is **NOT a social media growth project.** It is a *positioning*
project. The objective is durable credibility for two distinct
audiences (Track A: recruiters / school leaders; Track B: teachers /
peer practitioners) at a cost Mahmoud can sustain next to EDU-VLE
development.

Three CMO-level theses drive every choice below:

1. **Hero metric is not engagement.** It is *credibility signal per
   hour invested.* One LinkedIn post that produces one school-leader
   DM beats ten posts that produce 200 empty likes.
2. **Strategic compression beats abundance.** Most "social media
   strategies" assume a 5–15 hr/week budget with a team. At ~1
   hr/week, the only winning move is publishing fewer things, each
   of which compounds across both tracks.
3. **Authenticity beats production value.** A Vite screenshot of the
   working tutor, even unedited, beats a Canva deck.

## 2. Brand positioning

**Mahmoud's brand (Track A):**
> *A practising IGCSE/A-Level Physics teacher who also ships a
> production-grade AI tutor to teach his own craft the way he
> teaches it.*

**EDU-VLE's brand (Track B):**
> *A RAG-based physics tutor that cites the page it quotes from,
> built by a teacher who couldn't find an AI tutor that taught the
> way he does.*

These are *the same story* from two angles. They reinforce each
other. They never contradict, and the marketing surface area is
kept small on purpose.

### Non-goals (explicit)

EDU-VLE is **not** positioned for:
- End-student adoption (Golden Dataset = 1 worksheet, no scale).
- Multi-tenant school deployment (out of Session 4B scope).
- Adaptive difficulty / multi-language / multi-subject (out of
  scope; do not promise).
- MENA consumer launch or paid-acquisition play.

## 4. Content pillars (3, locked)

These three pillars carry all output. Every post belongs to exactly
one.

### Pillar 1 — *Teaching craft distilled*
- Audience: Track A primary.
- Voice: clinician-warm. Specific, grounded in classroom evidence.
- Example topics:
  - A workshop-style intro line that moved pass rates 11 points.
  - A worksheet redesign decision explained in 6 sentences.
  - An Edexcel spec-point walkthrough with a teacher-side tip.
- Cadence: 2x/month.
- Why first: this is the only pillar a school leader *reads without
  technical background*. It is also the pillar a recruiter quotes in
  a hiring meeting.

### Pillar 2 — *Build-in-public engineering*
- Audience: Track B primary, Track A secondary.
- Voice: engineer-to-engineer. Honest about bugs. Numbers, not vibes.
- Example topics:
  - A hybrid-retrieval trade-off with reasoning.
  - A QA finding from a Session 4 report, translated.
  - "Why I rejected X architecture for EDU-VLE."
- Cadence: 2x/month.
- Multiplier role: doesn't substitute Pillar 1 — it adds *and
  technically literate* on top. The two compounds together.

### Pillar 3 — *AI literacy + the future of pedagogy*
- Audience: both, sparingly.
- Voice: senior practitioner, not futurist. Reference real-world
  evidence (Edexcel, Ofqual, Cambridge, peer-reviewed) where
  possible.
- Cadence: 1x/month.
- 6+ month compound play; over-promise here and the brand breaks.

## 5. Channel posture

**One non-optional channel: LinkedIn.**

Every other surface is *output*, not *strategy*:
- GitHub repo (EDU-VLE) is the artifact, not a channel.
- A future personal site is a portfolio reader, if/when cheap.
- X/Twitter, YouTube, etc. exist only if a single LinkedIn-native
  asset cheaply republishes.

No paid ads. No newsletter platform. No SEO site. No short-form
video at this stage. Anything that requires >20 min to write or
that won't survive a 1-week pause fails the budget formally.

## 7. Tone of voice (locked)

### On-voice (these go in posts):
- "I rebuilt that worksheet intro on Tuesday. Pass rate moved 11
  points. The line is now: '[line].'"
- "Hybrid retrieval got rejected by the test harness today; the
  threshold value was wrong. Fix took one line."
- "Three recruiters told me last quarter they couldn't place
  engineers-into-education in their ATS. I think that's fixable."

### Off-voice (these do NOT):
- "Excited to announce…"
- "Honoured to share…"
- "Reflection:" / "Here's a thought:" openers.
- "I learned so much…" with no concrete datum.
- Any emoji-emoji-emoji opener pattern.
- "LinkedIn friends" or "my network."
- "Would love your thoughts!" as the only CTA.
- "Open to work" wording in body.
- "Drop a 🚀 if…" — never.

## 8. Success metrics (named, minimal)

Track A (primary):
- **Inbound DMs from recruiters or school leaders**: target ≥ 1
  qualified inbound / month by month 6; ≥ 1 / fortnight by month
  12.
- **Profile views from target-segment users**: ≥ +50% by month 3.
- **Profile-to-DM conversion**: ≥ 2% of profile viewers DMing.

Track B (secondary):
- **GitHub stars on EDU-VLE**: ≥ 50 by month 6 (modest, real).
- **"How did you build X" DM traffic from peers**: ≥ 2/month by
  month 4.
- **Teacher peers resharing Pillar 1 posts**: qualitative, no
  vanity number.

What is **not** a metric (explicitly refused):
- Follower count.
- Impressions.
- Engagement rate.
- "Reach."

These all reward volume, which the strategy refuses.

## 9. Hard guardrails (must-hold)

1. Marketing never consumes more than ~1 hour/week of total effort.
2. Marketing never interferes with EDU-VLE development sessions.
3. No fabricated claim: no MAU, no school count, no download
   count. Show what actually exists. The Golden Dataset is enough.
4. British English throughout.
5. No social handles, email, Telegram QR, or phone numbers in any
   classroom-material-style image (existing rule). In posts, a
   direct DM CTA only — never a "follow @handle."
6. Calendar pauses during EDU-VLE session weeks. A missed
   Tuesday is fine; a missed Tuesday that triggers a chain of
   "catch-up" posts is not. Reset cadence, do not back-fill.

## 10. Out-of-scope for M1 (this doc)

- No new channels are being tested.
- No product launch, no demo day, no announcement arc.
- No cohort, classroom, or pilot judgements until Session 4 is
  fully complete.
- No content-agreement or co-marketing with any external party.

The strategy *is* the work. The work is publishing.
