# LINKEDIN_STRATEGY.md

The single-channel execution layer of the marketing architecture
(see MARKETING_ARCHITECTURE.md). All LinkedIn work below happens
under the hard caps (≤1 hr/week total, ≤1 long-form post/week, no
fabrication, British English).

---

## 1. Weekly posting framework

Aliased to MARKETING_ARCHITECTURE.md §6. Operationally:

| Day (Tripoli) | Time | Pillar | Track bias | Pre-req |
|---------------|------|--------|------------|---------|
| Tuesday | 08:00 | P2 (odd weeks) / P1 (even weeks) | Track B / A | Pre-published comment seeding (5 substantive comments, Monday afternoon) |
| Thursday | 08:00 | P3 (≈1x/quarter only) | Both | Pre-published comment seeding |

**Pause rule.** During EDU-VLE "session" weeks (Sessions 4 +, where a
session is a planned multi-day build cycle), posting is *paused
without compensation*. A clean skip beats a rushed post that
undermines the brand.

**Recovery rule.** After a missed week(s), reset — do not back-fill
multiple posts in one week to "catch up." The strategy fails loud
and early if you do.

## 2. Post-archetype library (5 locked)

Each archetype below has hook variants ready-to-use. Only one hook
variant per post is used; the others are kept in a private
notebook for rotation.

### A1 — Engineering-decision (Pillar 2, Track B / A)

```text
Hook A (curiosity gap):
"I threw away 3 days of work on EDU-VLE yesterday. Here's the bug I
almost shipped to recruiters."

Hook B (specific story):
"Tuesday 11pm. I'm staring at a hybrid-retrieval score that doesn't
add up. Then I open the embedding notebook."

Hook C (bold claim):
"Most RAG demos fail on real classroom worksheets. Mine doesn't.
Here's the test that proved it."
```

Body: situation → tension → the engineering trade-off you resolved
→ a one-sentence pedagogical reflection at the close. The closing
pedagogy link is what makes Track B content *also* serve Track A.

CTA: "If you're a teacher using AI tools in your classroom, what's
your 'almost shipped a bug' story?"

### A2 — Teaching-craft (Pillar 1, Track A primary)

```text
Hook A:
"The moment I realised my Edexcel students couldn't read graphs
wasn't when they failed Paper 2. It was when I asked one to point
to the y-axis and she said 'the bottom one.'"

Hook B:
"Three free PDF worksheets got 12,000 downloads last year. The
underrated decision was the third one — here's why."

Hook C:
"I rewrote my IGCSE worksheet intro to one line. Pass rate went
up 11 points. Here's the line."
```

Body: classroom observation → pattern noticed → small change →
measurable result (named, rough, but never fabricated — see
MARKETING_ARCHITECTURE.md guardrail 3).

CTA: "What's a teaching change you made that worked but you'd never
catch in an obs lesson?"

### A3 — Career-trajectory, honest (Pillar 1, sparingly)

```text
Hook A:
"I have a mechanical engineering degree, a PGCEi half-finished,
and an AI tutor I built for my own students. Recruiters can't
categorise me. That's the point."

Hook B:
"A school leader asked me last week why a Mech. Eng. graduate is
teaching physics. Here's my answer."

Hook C:
"I failed three rounds of interviews for senior physics roles
last year. The thing that wasn't on my CV turned out to be the
solution."
```

Body: non-linear trajectory → specific moment of friction → what
unlocked the next opportunity. The "rare intersection" frame is
the strategic asset here.

CTA: "What's the non-linear path that brought you to where you
are?"

### A4 — Education-observation essay (Pillar 3)

```text
Hook A:
"Every EdTech pitch I've read in the last year has the same line:
'engagement first.' I think that's why teachers stop using them."
```

Body: one observation about EdTech/AI-in-education → counter-
evidence from classroom reality → one concrete implication for
someone shipping education products.

CTA: "What's one thing EdTech gets wrong about teachers?"

### A5 — EDU-VLE update card (Pillar 2, Track B artefact)

```text
Hook A:
"EDU-VLE session N update: 21 chunks, 1 worksheet, a tutor that
cites the page it quotes from. Today we [one specific milestone]."
```

Body: short, factual, screenshot-or-not (≤4 lines), milestone-only.
The weekly ritual that compounds Track B visibility naturally.

CTA: "Building in public for teachers and recruiters — follow for
the journey. Once-a-week update, no hype."

## 3. Carousel playbook (≤1x/fortnight)

Used *only* when a post has **reference value** — the post
deserves to be saved and re-found.

**Format (6-slide PDF, uploaded native):**
- **S1:** Hook as carousel cover (must function standalone — even
  if they never swipe).
- **S2–S5:** One insight per slide, max 15 words, one visual per
  slide.
- **S6:** A specific CTA tied to the carousel topic + follow prompt
  + "save for the next time you evaluate a RAG claim."

**Design budget:** ≤45 min. Build Wednesday evening if EDU-VLE dev
is paused for the day, never before. If a carousel exceeds 45
minutes, demote it to a long-form post.

## 4. Engagement-loop (the daily activity)

This is the **only daily marketing commitment**. Hard 15-min cap.
Anything past 15 minutes means the strategy is broken — log it and
adjust next week.

| Slot | Cadence | Action | Time cap |
|------|---------|--------|----------|
| Pre-publish | Monday afternoon | 5 substantive comments on target-recruiter and target-educator accounts | 15 min |
| Post-publish | First 60 min after posting | Respond to every comment (substantive first, then questions) | 15 min |
| Daily | Any 15-min window | 2–3 substantive comments on 2–3 target-account posts | 15 min |

**Target-account list (refresh quarterly):**
- 10 international-school Heads of Physics in Gulf + Egypt + UK.
- 5 EdTech-recruitment accounts (in-house + headhunters).
- 5 peer-teacher accounts in Edexcel network.
- 5 EdTech-builder accounts.

Personalised comments only. Never use the default "Great post!"
boilerplate. Aim for "I added one new thing to your point, here it
is."

## 5. Recruiter-focused content (Track A amplifier)

Two specific patterns for posts whose primary audience is
recruiters, even when disguised as Pillar 1 content:

1. **The named-result pattern.** "Pass rate went up 11 points" is
   the strongest single signal in Track A. Recruiters recognise
   named, defensible numbers. The rule: *only use this when the
   number is real*. The brand dies if a single number is
   fabricated by even 1%.
2. **The non-linear pattern.** Engineering → teaching → AI is the
   brand's moat. Each post that lands "I came from this, now I
   do this" adds the trajectory signal recruiters verify but
   cannot find in a CV.

**Recruiter-first FAQ (DM prep):**
When a recruiter DMs after reading a Pillar 1 post, the response
should:
- Acknowledge by referencing the exact post.
- Offer one concrete artifact (a screenshot, a worksheet, a code
  snippet) — *not* a CV.
- Ask one quallifying question to verify fit.

Recruiter-DM response budget: ≤10 min each, ≤3/week.

## 6. Educational / teaching reflection content (Track B amplifier)

- Workshop-disguised-as-post pattern: "Here's what I tried on
  Tuesday, here's the result, here's the next adjustment." The
  'next adjustment' framing is the giveaway that it's workshop-
  grade material, not boast-grade material.
- Spec-point teardowns (Track B's specific moat for IGCSE/A-Level
  teachers): "Here is spec point 1.3, what students miss, and one
  worksheet prompt to fix it." This format saves well and travels
  inside teacher networks.

## 7. Technical insights content (Track A enabler)

Track A is served *transitively* by Track B technical insights,
because Track B rigour transfers. But:

- Posts in this category **must** include the pedagogical
  reflection at the close (per A1 archetype). Otherwise, this
  category leaks into "exhausting-engineer-navel-gaze," which
  *fails* Track A.
- Frequency lock: max 2x/month (Pillar 2 cadence).
- Never post a tutorial architecture screenshot without an
  ack that EDU-VLE is still WIP. Avoid the "I shipped it!" over-
  claim that PMM guarded against.

## 8. Tagging, links, hashtags

- 3–5 specific hashtags per post. Starter set:
  `#IGCSEPhysics`, `#EdexcelPhysics`, `#AlevelPhysics`, `#EdTech`,
  `#AITutoring`. Drop any that don't earn impressions over a
  quarter; never >5.
- No external links in body. Always link-in-comments. (LinkedIn
  algorithm actively penalises link-in-body for non-company
  accounts.)
- Tags only when genuinely relevant. Peer teachers for
  collaboration posts; recruiters in target networks sparingly;
  the EDU-VLE GitHub link when relevant.

## 9. Style rules recap (locked, all posts)

- British English.
- One post = one idea.
- ≤3 lines per paragraph (white space is engagement).
- CTA invites a reply, not a broadcast.
- Hook in the first line, every time.
- No fabricated numbers, references, schools, students, or
  outcomes.
- No Telegram/email/phone/handle in classroom-style imagery
  (per existing brand rule); DM CTA only in posts.

## 10. LinkedIn profile as the deliverable

The profile is the conversion target — posts are evidence streams
that prove the profile's claims. Optimisation order:
1. **Headline.** `Physics Teacher (Edexcel IGCSE & A-Level) ·
   Building EDU-VLE, an AI tutor I teach with · Software engineer
   turned educator`. (Identity + niche + active proof + trajectory.)
2. **About section.** Hook → what he does (teaches + builds) →
   EDU-VLE in one paragraph (Track B proof surface) →
   trajectory paragraph → CTA with DM keyword ("DM
   'EDU-VLE' to talk").
3. **Featured section.** Manual curation, not chronological.
   Order: best demo screenshot → best craft post → best
   engineering-decision post → best teaching-failure post → best
   selectivity / rejection post → EDU-VLE landing post.
4. **Banner / photo.** Plain professional photo. No retouching
   cycles. Banner is a single sentence, e.g. "I teach physics and
   I build the tutor I wish I had."

**Profile refresh cadence:** once per quarter, max. Posts prove
the profile. The profile does not change weekly — that signals
insecurity.

## 11. Loop / measurement

Two weekly reads:
- Profile views (LinkedIn native analytics).
- Inbound DMs by source (post-driven vs connection-driven).

One monthly read:
- Hire-pipeline conversations tracked privately (each row: who,
  role, how they reached out, outcome).

No vanity dashboards. No follower count obsession. The strategy
returns value only against the metrics in
MARKETING_ARCHITECTURE.md §8.
